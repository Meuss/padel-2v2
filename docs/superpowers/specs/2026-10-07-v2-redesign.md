# Meuss Padel Club V2: redesign spec

Agreed on 2026-10-07 in a design interview. Product truth is in `PRODUCT.md`, the
glossary in `CONTEXT.md`. V2 is built with Claude Opus 5.5. The README shows V1 and
V2 side by side.

## Direction

- **Look:** Pro Tour Broadcast, the standard look done properly. A night arena
  with floodlights, a black steel and tinted-glass cage, a blue court, a crowd in
  the stands, and LED perimeter boards.
- **Bar:** Premier Padel broadcast graphics for the HUD and between-point
  graphics. Top Spin 2K25 for the court, lighting and camera.
- **Approved layout comp:** `docs/design/v2-comp-clean-feed.png` (local working copy: `.impeccable/mocks/comp-1-minimal.png`).
  During the rally the only HUD element is a score bug in the bottom-left. The
  arena carries the rest.
- **Also adopted:** the between-points Banner and the REPLAY tag from
  `comp-3-between-points.png`, and the Timing arc from `comp-2-full-broadcast.png`,
  shown for about 0.6 s after each Shot.
- **Name:** Meuss Padel Club. No company branding. The Teams are **Azul** and
  **Rojo** in all user-facing text. _Superseded 2026-10-08: English copy except team names._
- **Platform:** desktop only. 60 fps at 1080p on a MacBook Air M1 or equivalent
  integrated GPU, with an automatic lower-quality fallback.

## Stages (each one ships playable and passes `pnpm typecheck && pnpm test && pnpm build`)

1. **Foundation**
   - Fix the HTML injection through nicknames, and sanitise names on the server.
   - Fix the court being duplicated on reconnect.
   - Client-side prediction with reconciliation for the local player's movement.
   - Only seated players may add or clear bots.
   - The server enforces `PROTOCOL_VERSION`, and outdated clients see a
     "New version, reload" screen.
   - V1/V2 framing in the README.
2. **World**
   - The broadcast arena: floodlights, cage, LED boards (club name, live score,
     nicknames, reactions), and an instanced crowd that cheers on points.
   - Rigged low-poly GLTF players (Quaternius or Mixamo class, CC0) with idle,
     run, swing and smash animations, and nicknames on the shirts.
   - A Player cam behind your own Side with long-lens broadcast framing. The
     Broadcast cam for Spectators.
   - The V2 README screenshot is taken from the real game at the end of this
     stage or later, never from a mockup.
3. **Feel**
   - Shots: Drive is left-click, Lob is right-click, and Smash is automatic
     when the ball is high at contact.
   - Timing (early, perfect, late) changes power and accuracy.
   - The Serve is aimed, and its Timing decides between a clean serve and a
     Fault. Random faults are removed.
   - Bots use all Shot types at one fixed skill level.
   - A full CC0 sound set: crowd ambience that swells on long rallies, ball
     pops, glass thuds, net cord, and the Score call shown as text.
   - Hit feedback, including the Timing arc.
4. **Broadcast layer**
   - The score bug, matching the comp exactly.
   - The Banner between points.
   - An Instant replay of notable points that everyone sees. Any Player can skip
     it, and it never delays the Serve.
   - Nickname tags that show only between points, plus a subtle marker under
     your own player.
   - The Final card, then an automatic Rematch vote.
   - A "Take seat" button: it replaces a Bot or fills an empty Seat, between
     points.

## Out of scope

Mobile and touch, a bot difficulty setting, a spoken Score call, accounts, and more
than one room.
